# Figure 1g, version 025

## Methods

Compounds were selected directly from `Broad_8K_Antibiotic_Activity_Dataset_Filtered_.csv` using the supplied binary activity labels. The older Figure 2d property table was not used. Molecular structures were parsed with RDKit 2025.09.1 and identified by canonical isomeric SMILES. Stereochemistry and disconnected fragments were retained. Duplicate structures were combined using logical OR for positive activity calls within each strain. No compound was excluded because of a missing continuous growth-inhibition measurement.

Each strain group contains every compound with a positive binary activity label in that strain. The groups contain 8 WT, 5 Δ40, 39 Δ3, and 124 Δ6 structures. Compounds active in multiple strains appear in every corresponding group. No exclusions are applied based on activity in another strain. All eight WT hits are also Δ3 and Δ6 hits, and all 39 Δ3 hits are Δ6 hits. Four WT hits are also Δ40 hits. The four groups contain 176 observations representing 125 unique structures. Figure labels use only WT, Δ40, Δ3, and Δ6 with their sample sizes. No inactive comparison group is shown.

Topological polar surface area (TPSA) was calculated with `rdMolDescriptors.CalcTPSA(mol, includeSandP=False)`. LogP was calculated with `Crippen.MolLogP`. Amine count is the total number of primary, secondary, and tertiary amine nitrogen atoms, including protonated forms and amines attached to aromatic carbon atoms. Aromatic ring nitrogens, amides, thioamides, sulfonamides, amidines, guanidines, cyanamides, imines, nitriles, nitro groups, amine oxides, and quaternary ammonium groups are excluded. The exact query is provided in `methods/amine_descriptor.py`, along with 33 functional-group checks. This descriptor counts structural amine sites and does not predict their protonation state at assay pH. Separate primary, secondary, and tertiary amine counts are included in the data table.

Boxes show the first and third quartiles, with a line at the median. Whiskers reach the most extreme observations within 1.5 interquartile ranges of the box. Every compound is overlaid with identical marker styling and a deterministic horizontal jitter rule. Box-plot outlier markers are suppressed because those observations are already included in the complete point layer. No vertical jitter or point sampling is used. Values are not clipped. Observed ranges are 0 to 206.07 square ångströms for TPSA, 0 to 3 for amine count, and approximately 0.230 to 7.634 for logP. The axes span −8 to 220, −0.2 to 3.25, and 0 to 8, respectively. Small margins below zero for TPSA and amine count keep zero-valued points fully visible. The original green, orange, red, and purple strain colours are retained.

Group membership was checked independently using sets of positively labelled source records. Descriptor values for the 124 structures shared with the previously verified hit table were checked against that table. The additional Δ40 structure was calculated using the same descriptor functions. The plotting audit checks all 528 points across the three panels.

Only panel g is replaced in the Illustrator document. The open version 024 document is used when available so existing edits are carried forward. Exports of that document immediately before and after the panel replacement are compared to verify that panel f and all other artwork outside panel g are unchanged. The other figure panels are retained. The original supplied Illustrator file and version 024 remain available.

## Caption

**g**, Physicochemical properties of active compounds in WT, Δ40, Δ3, and Δ6. The groups contain all compounds with a positive binary activity label in the corresponding strain, giving n = 8, 5, 39, and 124, respectively. Compounds active in multiple strains appear in each corresponding group. Activity is defined by the supplied binary labels at 80% growth inhibition. The panels show topological polar surface area (TPSA, $\mathrm{\AA}^2$), number of primary, secondary, and tertiary amines combined, and Wildman–Crippen logP. Amine counts include protonated forms and exclude amides and aromatic ring nitrogens. Boxes show interquartile ranges, central lines show medians, and whiskers extend to the most extreme observations within 1.5 interquartile ranges of the box. Every compound is shown as a point using the same horizontal jitter rule and marker style in all groups. Points may overlap, especially for integer amine counts. All values are included within the displayed axes. Green, orange, red, and purple denote WT, Δ40, Δ3, and Δ6, respectively.

## Reproduction

`methods/reproduce_all_hit_property_panel.py` generates the data tables, checks the cohort definitions, and writes the three-property panel as PDF, SVG, and PNG. `methods/integrate_all_hits_in_illustrator.jsx` imports the panel into the existing panel g region of version 024 and saves `Figure1_v025_all_hits_TPSA_amines_LogP.ai`. `methods/export_all_hit_figure.py` exports a matching vector PDF and 300 dpi review PDF from the saved native Illustrator file. It checks the property labels, counts, fonts, and unchanged artwork outside panel g, including an exact comparison of panel f. All code uses absolute input and output paths and requires no command-line arguments.
